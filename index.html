Subject
Formal Amendment of the Annual Anniversary Celebration Date Pertaining to the Event Colloquially Known as "Sinagot na"

This memorandum is issued by mutual understanding and with full affection to formally record, acknowledge, and effect the amendment of the officially observed anniversary date for the aforementioned occasion. All relevant parties are hereby informed, and no further deliberation is required.

It has been determined, through careful consideration of scheduling, sentiment, and the general logistics of love, that the date previously designated for the annual commemoration of Sinagot na shall no longer be observed on its original date. The previous date of August 18 is hereby retired from active service, with gratitude for its tenure.

Previously Observed On
August 18
→
Henceforth Observed On
February 25
Effective this year and all years to follow, without exception, excuse, or forgetfulness.

The occasion to be commemorated — that singular, momentous, and rather brave act of pagsagot — remains unchanged in its significance and weight. Only the calendar date of its annual observance has been revised. The love, the memory, and the mild embarrassment of recounting the story remain fully intact and non-amendable.

All parties are expected to honor February 25 accordingly: with celebration, remembrance, and the appropriate acknowledgment that someone said yes. Any failure to observe the amended date may be cited in future negotiations. This memo shall serve as the official and binding record of said amendment.
